# QuadLoom 0.4.4 — thin parts

**Not released on its own.** 0.4.4 was built and tested but not published; its changes ship in [0.4.6](0.4.6.md), the first public release after 0.4.0. The artifact named below was never distributed.

## What is new (decision [0026](../decisions/0026-thin-parts.md))

- **Thin tubes keep their shape.** A tail, a finger or a wire gets at least twelve triangle edges round it in the prep remesh, where the thinnest had five, so a layout can be traced on it.
- **Small holes stay open.** The same rule holds for the wall of a hole (`thingi10k/906891`: 1.31% of the diagonal off its input to between 0.24% and 0.39%).
- **At least six quads round a thin part, at any Density or Adaptive Detail.** At Low Poly's quad size the pig's tail was a square bar of four quads; it now has eight round it, and the body's quads grade into it.
- Flat thin things (an ear, a strap, a blade), the rounded rim of a plate and a bar with sharp edges are left as they were: they are not round, and one quad across is right for them.
- **Relaxing is faster on models with many patches**, with the same results: BrainStem's run goes from 11.8 s to 5.6 s. The search that puts a vertex back on its own patch had no bound to start from, which also made one model take two minutes once its pinched quads were free to move (below).
- No new settings. The add-on's API version is still 11.

## What to expect

- At the default settings most models look as they did. Over the 70 benchmark models, quad shape and the largest distance from the input are within what a run varies by on its own; the mean distance is better on 24 models and worse on 9; there are 2.8% more quads. Ten models timed back to back take 17% less time than with 0.4.3 (142 s to 118 s), from the faster relaxing; a model whose thin parts gain many quads can take longer (`thingi10k/906891`: 4 s to 10 s, with its holes kept).
- The change shows on models with thin round parts, and more the coarser the quads.

## Known limits

- With Symmetry on, a part the mirror plane cuts in half (a tail or a nose on the plane) gets the finer triangles and not the six-quad rule.
- SunglassesKhronos' lens frames (bars thinner than a quad, with sharp edges) can come out with a strip kept as triangles. That is the open "slender patches" item; this version's finer triangles on thin parts happen to change which layout that model gets.

## A finding that matters more than the feature

The same build, run with a change that means nothing (the remesh tolerance times 1.0005), gives a different result on most models: a model's worst quads move by 0.02 at the median and by up to 0.15, and one model went from 0.33% to 1.01% off its input. 0.4.3 itself is better than QRemeshify on 51 of 60 models as built and on 48 with that nudge.

So no single run can judge a change, and several earlier "wins" and "losses" on single models were chance. Builds are now scored as pairs (`quad_eval.py --twin`, `quad_pairs.py` (development tools, not in the public repository)). It also means the next gain in quality is likely in the layout's stability, or in trying a few layouts and keeping the best, more than in any one rule.

## Through the add-on

All 70 benchmark models complete ([report](../../benchmark/reports/20261127-q044-default/README.md)). Against QRemeshify, over the 60 models it finishes: mean scaled Jacobian better on 49, Hausdorff distance on 46 (0.4.2: 51 and 42). One result is further than 1% from its input (0.4.2: two). The run takes 630 s of tool time against 0.4.2's 690 s. These are single runs: see the finding above for how far to read them.
