# QuadLoom 0.4.2 — Sizes that fit the parts, straighter strips, a setting for faint creases

**Not released on its own.** 0.4.2 was built and tested but not published; its changes ship in [0.4.6](0.4.6.md), the first public release after 0.4.0. The artifact named below was never distributed.

## What changed (decision [0024](../decisions/0024-size-per-part-strip-relaxing.md))

- **Each part gets its own size.** Flat, open and many-part models no longer get runaway counts (a flat 1,267-triangle figure: 144,905 quads to 8,761), and parts whose feature lines lie close together get finer quads (Corset: mean scaled Jacobian 0.882 to 0.948).
- **Thin strips no longer lean.** A row of quads between two feature lines lines up across the strip, and small quads hold their shape beside large ones.
- **A part can no longer vanish.** A patch with far more area than its boundary can span keeps its triangles (BrainStem had a whole part missing: 3.2% off its input, now 0.3%).
- **New setting: Ignore Faint Creases** (under Sharp Edges; off by default). Short or shallow crease lines stop steering the edge flow, for straighter flow on smooth surfaces. Right-angle edges, marked sharp edges, seams and borders are always kept. Try 4 to 9 on a sculpt with fur, pores or scan noise.
- The add-on's API version is 10 (the new setting and the `faint_edges` report key): restart Blender after installing over an older version.
